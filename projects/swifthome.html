01

The problem

Starting over in a new city means furnishing from scratch.

Moving across borders, international students arrive in unfamiliar cities and empty apartments. They need a place that feels like home without the burden of buying, transporting, and later discarding furniture.

How might wehelp first-year graduate international students living off campus furnish, and move out of, their apartments quickly without spending too much time or money?
02

Research

Listening to people mid-move.

15interviewees aged 20–30
13survey questions on moving & furnishing
2rounds of interviews

02 What we learned

Who we were designing for.

01020304

Insight 01 / 04Insight 02 / 04Insight 03 / 04Insight 04 / 04

Most international students don’t have a car, and they prefer to do everything on their phones.

Their furniture needs are diverse and long-term, and they want urgent delivery with some flexibility.

They value recyclability, but don’t require it.

Newly arrived, they feel anxious about furnishing and moving out, and turn to social media for help.

03

Ideation

Seven lenses on one service.

After brainstorming, we pushed ideas through SCAMPER. Tap a letter.

Substitute

  • Real-life furniture photos and AR models instead of static displays.
  • Partner with logistics companies to cut transport costs.

Combine

  • Bundle furniture rental with transport to ease moving.
  • One-stop move-in and move-out for a seamless, closed loop.

Adapt

  • Airbnb-style room recommendations: packages by room type and household size.
  • Feather’s “the longer the lease, the lower the rent”.
  • A takeaway-style tracker: order → delivery → installation.

Modify

  • Drag a timeline to set the rental period.
  • Tap an image to view furniture in AR, 360°.
  • A warm colour palette for visual comfort.

Put to other use

  • Extend to university orientation, helping first-years decorate their dorms.

Eliminate

  • Automatic cost calculation, shown visually.
  • One-click sign-up.
  • Only show furniture available in the delivery area.

Rearrange

  • Put furniture recommendations before address verification, so browsing comes first.
