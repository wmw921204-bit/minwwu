My role

I guided the team from research to a tested redesign, turning interview insights into the priorities that shaped every iteration.

  • Research synthesis & strategy
  • Framework & interaction design
  • Low- to high-fidelity prototyping
01

Vision

What if a Brilliant.org student could stay consistently engaged?

02

Research

Learners want engaging ways to close their STEM gaps.

Our audience: students in higher education actively trying to fill accumulated knowledge gaps.

35%dropout risk post-pandemic among learners with a bachelor’s degree or higher
