Level 1Vision

What if a Brilliant learner could stay engaged?

Level 2Research

Learners want engaging ways to close their STEM gaps.

Our audience: students in higher education actively trying to fill accumulated knowledge gaps.

Quick question

Since the pandemic, what share of learners with a bachelor’s degree or higher are at risk of dropping out?

Level 4Ideation & iteration

From ideas to a tested flow, one level at a time.

Level 5The design

Meet the redesign, one feature at a time.

Home & streaks

The home page opens on your streak and today’s lesson, so coming back becomes a routine.

Learning path

A course reads as a path of lessons, so learners always know where they are and what comes next.

Course overview

An overview and pre-test before each course, with rollover text explaining every lesson widget.

Goals & review

Daily goals pay out XP, and the review panel tracks activity, goals and mistakes in one place.

Mistake review

Every wrong answer waits in a review deck, ready to retry with an explanation one click away.

Pick a time

Learners schedule lessons on a calendar and get reminded by email or phone.

Ask Koji

An AI tutor sits beside every problem, answering questions and explaining in more detail.

Level 6Usability tests

Five flows, five test runs.

  1. Course overview
  2. Set a goal
  3. Mistake correction
  4. Calendar goal
  5. Ask Koji

Total XP0

Course overview

Room to grow
70%Idea success
76%Action success
85%Satisfaction

Learners flipped through lesson content without trying the questions, but the detailed blurbs and rollover text made them more confident choosing a course.

Learner’s ideaShow the lesson widgets up front instead of asking questions.

Rollover text won people over. The questions, not yet.

Set a goal

Nailed it
100%Idea success
80%Action success
70%Satisfaction

Everyone understood goals as a way to measure success. Some went through several menus first, and it was unclear whether goals were set per lesson until the task was done.

Learner’s ideaTie goals to XP, leaderboards and other rewards.

Goals clicked right away. Finding them took a while.

Mistake correction

Nailed it
100%Idea success
88%Action success
80%Satisfaction

Learners found their mistakes quickly in the review panel, corrected all of them and read most explanations.

Learner’s ideaRetest failed content with similar questions, and give a little XP for each fix.

All mistakes fixed! XP for that, please.

Calendar goal

Room to grow
93%Idea success
70%Action success
76%Satisfaction

Picking a daily task was easy. Replacing a goal in progress pushed straight through the warning, and unevenly sized buttons bothered people, though they appreciated being warned.

Learner’s ideaUse cosmetics as an easy reward for collectors.

Careful! That goal wasn’t finished.

Ask Koji

Room to grow
66%Idea success
70%Action success
76%Satisfaction

Learners enjoyed exploring different perspectives with Koji, but struggled to phrase questions that it answered well.

Learner’s ideaAn FAQ chatbot that links to the same articles as the rollover text.

Asking me good questions is harder than it looks.
