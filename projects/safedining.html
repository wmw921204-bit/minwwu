04

Ecosystem analysis

Many players, shared responsibility.

  1. 01 · Micro

    Trust begins with people

    Diners, friends and family, servers, cooks and managers decide in the moment whether a meal is safe.

  2. 02 · Meso

    The support network

    Suppliers, certifiers, review platforms and ordering software decide whether allergy information is carried, checked and passed on.

  3. 03 · Macro

    The system conditions

    Regulators, laws, delivery platforms and advocacy groups set the rules everyone else works within.

05 Identified problems

Five orders the system keeps getting wrong.

53.9%44.8%74%44%?

Problem 01 / 05Allergy

Lost in conversation

Allergy details travel by word of mouth, from diner to server to chef. Each retelling invites error, and 53.9% of reactions happen after the diner has spoken up.

— — — — — — — — — —

Problem 02 / 05Allergy

The illusion of transparency

44.8% of restaurants had no ingredient lists or recipes for most of the menu. “Transparency” stops at a few vague labels.

— — — — — — — — — —

Problem 03 / 05Allergy

The anxiety of eating

Every order and double-check carries risk. 74% of parents of children with allergies find dining out challenging.

— — — — — — — — — —

Problem 04 / 05Allergy

Untrained to care

Only 44% of service staff get any allergy training, and 11.9% of managers believe “small amounts” are safe.

— — — — — — — — — —

Problem 05 / 05Allergy

The lost identity of ingredients

Ingredients start with clear origins and allergen data, then lose their names somewhere between supplier and plate.

06

Opportunity areas

Turn risk into confidence.

  1. #01

    Standard pathways

    Standardize communication pathways: make allergy communication a system, not a skill.

  2. #02

    Visible safety

    Make the invisible visible: menus, displays and cues that show safety in action.

  3. #03

    Trust before arrival

    Trust starts before you arrive: clear allergy-friendly information online, before the visit.

  4. #04

    Training as care

    Reimagine training as a culture of care, with real scenarios and real-time feedback.

  5. #05

    Traceable ingredients

    Let safety travel with every ingredient, through one shared data language from supplier to plate.

  6. #06

    One framework

    One framework for regulation and accountability, across suppliers, platforms and restaurants.
